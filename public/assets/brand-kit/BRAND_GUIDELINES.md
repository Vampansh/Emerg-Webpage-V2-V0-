## 1. Brand Visual Overview

The **EMERG** brand visual identity is an authentic mark that balances high-tech minimalism with dimensional depth:
* **Symbol:** A stylized lowercase `e` with an energetic diagonal slash cutting across and extending past the upper-right circle edge, shadowed by an offset **Lilac Mist** crescent arc to the lower-left.
* **Wordmark:** **EMERG** in architectural, geometric uppercase sans-serif letterforms.
* **Tagline:** **Unfold Possibilities** in clean, human-centered sentence case typography.

## 6. Clear Space & Size Guidelines

### Clear Space ($X$-Unit)
* Keep a clear exclusion margin around the logo equal to the height of the letter **`E`** in EMERG.
* No text, icons, buttons, or graphics should intrude into this boundary.

### Minimum Sizes
* **Digital:** Minimum `140 px` width for full logo; minimum `32 px` for symbol alone; `16 px` for `favicon.ico`.
* **Print:** Minimum `35 mm` width for full logo; minimum `10 mm` for symbol alone.

## 7. Brand Misuse & Guardrails ("Do's & Don'ts")

1. **DO NOT** stretch, squeeze, or distort the logo proportions.
2. **DO NOT** rotate the mark or change the diagonal angle.
3. **DO NOT** change the colors outside the approved palette.
4. **DO NOT** remove the lilac shadow arc when showing the full logo.
5. **DO NOT** add outer glows, artificial drop shadows, or bevel effects.
6. **DO NOT** place the dark logo on dark photographic backgrounds; use the inverted white master ([`emerg-logo-white.svg`](file:///Users/suryanshyadav/EMERG/%20EMERG%20AI/brand-kit/vectors/emerg-logo-white.svg) or [`emerg-logo-white-2k.png`](file:///Users/suryanshyadav/EMERG/%20EMERG%20AI/brand-kit/png-high-res/emerg-logo-white-2k.png)).
