Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Our work is custom and time-based, so refunds depend on how much work has been done. This policy applies unless your signed proposal says otherwise.

1. Project work (branding, websites, production)

  • Cancelled before work begins: the advance is refunded in full, less any third-party costs already paid on your behalf.
  • Cancelled after work begins: you pay for work completed up to the cancellation date. Any unused part of the advance is refunded.
  • After final delivery or launch: fees are not refundable.

2. Monthly retainers

Retainers can be cancelled with 30 days’ written notice. The current paid month is not refundable, and no further invoices are raised after the notice period.

3. Shoots and bookings

Photo and video shoots can be rescheduled free of charge with at least 48 hours’ notice. Shorter notice may carry a rescheduling fee to cover crew, location and equipment costs.

4. Non-refundable items

Ad spend paid to platforms, domains, hosting, software subscriptions, stock media and other third-party purchases cannot be refunded by EMERG.

5. How to request a refund

Email hello@emerg.co.in with your invoice number and reason. We reply within 3 working days, and approved refunds are sent to the original payment method within 7–10 working days.
