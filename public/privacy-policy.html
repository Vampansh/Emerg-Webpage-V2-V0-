Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you submit an inquiry through our contact form or communicate with EMERG via email or WhatsApp, we collect only the details you voluntarily provide: your full name, email address, phone number, selected service interest, and project message.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you share strictly to:

  • Respond to your project inquiries and consultation requests.
  • Prepare project proposals, scope documents, and service agreements.
  • Communicate updates regarding ongoing projects you engage us for.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal contact details with third-party marketers or data brokers.

3. Form Processing & Third-Party Services

Inquiries submitted on this website are delivered via FormSubmit to our official studio email address. Embedded social media previews (such as Instagram Reels) are served directly by Instagram and are subject to Meta Platforms' privacy practices when you interact with them. Our site also stores your chosen light/dark display theme preference locally in your browser (`localStorage`).

4. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain inquiry correspondence only for as long as necessary to follow up on your request or manage an active client relationship. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your inquiry data at any time by contacting us directly.
