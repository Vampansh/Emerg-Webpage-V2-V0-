Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how EMERG (“EMERG”, “we”, “us”), a digital marketing, media, web and AI studio based in Jabalpur, Madhya Pradesh, India, collects and handles personal data when you visit emerg.co.in or contact us. We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules.

1. Information we collect

  • Details you give us: name, email address, phone number, the service you are interested in, and your project message, when you use the contact form, email, phone or WhatsApp.
  • Client project material: brand assets, logins, content and business information you share with us while we deliver a paid engagement.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited, time of visit) collected by our hosting provider for security and performance.
  • Browser storage: your light/dark theme choice, stored only on your device using localStorage.

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 18. If you believe a child has sent us data, contact us and we will delete it.

2. Why we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and send proposals, quotes and contracts.
  • To deliver, manage and invoice the services you engage us for.
  • To keep the website secure, prevent spam and fix technical issues.
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We process your data on the basis of your consent (when you submit the form or message us) and for legitimate uses permitted under the DPDP Act. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we do not send marketing messages without your permission.

3. Who we share it with

We only share data with service providers who help us run the studio, and only as much as they need:

  • FormSubmit — delivers contact-form messages to our email inbox.
  • Vercel — hosts this website and may record anonymous, cookie-free visit statistics.
  • Google Fonts — serves the website typeface; your browser connects to Google to load it.
  • Email, WhatsApp and workspace tools we use to communicate and manage projects.

Some of these providers may store data outside India. We may also disclose data where required by law or a lawful order from a government authority.

4. Cookies

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics or advertising tools in the future, we will update this policy and ask for consent where required.

5. How long we keep it

Inquiries that do not become projects are deleted within 24 months. Client records and invoices are kept for as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (generally 8 years). Server logs are kept for a short period by our hosting provider.

6. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Access to client data is limited to the team members working on your project. No online system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of any breach as the law requires.

7. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it is used;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it, or withdraw consent you gave earlier;
  • name a person to exercise these rights on your behalf.

Email hello@emerg.co.in with the subject line “Data request”. We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
